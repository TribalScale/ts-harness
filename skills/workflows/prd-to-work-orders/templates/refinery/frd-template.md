```markdown
# [Feature Name]

**FRD ID:** FRD-NNN
**Parent Feature:** [FRD-NNN or None]
**Dependencies:** [FRD-NNN or None]
**Personas:** [Persona names]
**Business Problem Link:** [Pain point or business problem]

## Overview

[What the feature does, why users need it, and what value it delivers.]

## Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| [Term] | [Precise definition] |

## Requirements

### REQ-[PREFIX]-001: [Requirement Name]

**User Story:** As a [specific persona], I want to [action], so that I can [outcome].

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- **AC-[PREFIX]-001.1:** When [condition], the system shall [behavior].
```

## Rules

- FRDs are implementation-agnostic.
- Acceptance criteria are atomic and testable.
- Every requirement traces to a persona and business problem.
